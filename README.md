## Md Abdul Helal Kafy

Plant geneticist working at the interface of field-based quantitative genetics and computational genomics.

**Research interests:** genetic basis of crop traits and stress responses · RNA-seq and transcriptomics · gene families · quantitative genetics and mixed models

### Selected work
- **Rice ERF cross-stress expression atlas** — differential-expression analysis of 121 ERF genes across five public RNA-seq stress experiments (PyDESeq2), with QC, association tests and qRT-PCR comparison. Manuscript under review at *Genome*. → [rice-erf-stress-atlas](https://github.com/AbdulHelalKafy/rice-erf-stress-atlas)
- **Host-plant resistance in eggplant** — genetic variability, heritability (linear mixed models, lme4/sommer) and multivariate analysis of 30 genotypes against shoot and fruit borer. Kafy et al. (2025) *Discover Plants* 2:309. [doi:10.1007/s44372-025-00401-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44372-025-00401-2)

### Skills
**Languages:** Python (pandas, NumPy, BioPython), R (lme4, sommer, tidyverse, ggplot2), Unix shell
**Bioinformatics:** fastp, HISAT2, featureCounts, PyDESeq2, HMMER, MCScanX, TBtools, eggNOG-mapper
**Training:** variant calling (GATK, BCFtools), genome assembly, pan-genome analysis, Snakemake
**Lab:** qRT-PCR, replicated field trials (RCBD), UV–VIS biochemical assays
